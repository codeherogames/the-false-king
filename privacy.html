Privacy Policy

The False King: Chess Puzzles · Effective November 21, 2026

In short: you can play without giving us your name or email. We keep an anonymous account so your results, leaderboard places and purchases follow you. There are no ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

The False King: Chess Puzzles ("the app") is a chess puzzle game for iPhone. In this policy "we" means its developer. You can reach us at daejjd@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account. When the app first goes online it creates an account with a random ID. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or device identifier.
  • Your leaderboard name. A generated name such as "Swift Knight 4821", or the two words you choose for it in Settings. It is shown publicly on the leaderboards.
  • Your puzzle results and records. For the boards you solve: time, number of moves, hints used, tries and the date. These make up the leaderboards and your Puzzle IQ.
  • Sign in with Apple or Google, if you choose it. We receive an identifier for your Apple or Google account, and the email address Apple or Google shares with us (with Apple you can choose to hide your real address). We use it only to sign you in to the same account on another device. We never email you for marketing.
  • Purchases. Purchases are made through Apple. We, and RevenueCat, our purchase service, see which products you bought, when, and whether a subscription is active, linked to your account ID. We never see your payment details.
  • Usage events. To improve the game, the app records events such as a puzzle being completed, a hint being used or a purchase being started, with a random install ID and your account ID. They contain no name, email, location or contacts.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier or browsing history. The app shows no advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

On your device

Your puzzles, progress, settings and the boards you made in Create Mode are kept on your device. Boards you create are never uploaded.

How we use it

  • To keep your progress, records and purchases with your account.
  • To run the daily puzzle, the leaderboards and the Puzzle IQ.
  • To deliver the puzzle packs you bought.
  • To find out which features and puzzles players enjoy, and to fix problems.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your data. It is handled only by the services that run the app:

  • Supabase, which hosts accounts, results, leaderboards and puzzle packs.
  • RevenueCat, which manages purchases and subscriptions.
  • Apple, for the App Store, purchases and Sign in with Apple, and Google for Sign in with Google.

Your leaderboard name, times and scores are visible to other players. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. To delete it, open Settings › Account › Delete account in the app. This permanently removes your account, leaderboard name, results and records from our servers, and asks RevenueCat to delete its record of you. Usage events already recorded are kept without any link to you. Your progress stays on your device until you delete the app. You can also ask us by email to delete your data.

Purchases are recorded by Apple under your Apple ID, so you can restore them later with Restore purchases.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your data, and to object to how it is used. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
